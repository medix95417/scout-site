Two-Factor Authentication

Adds a second step to logging in. Required for accounts that hold Treasurer or unit-wide admin access, since those can see or move real money — optional, but recommended, for everyone else.

Choose either method, or set up both: a security key (a YubiKey or similar, or the passkey your phone or laptop offers) that you touch at login, or an authenticator app that shows a 6-digit code. Whichever you set up first also gives you backup codes for the day the key or phone isn't with you.
